How the numbers are calculated
xG. Logistic regression on 5,606 non-penalty shots from StatsBomb open data (World Cup 2018 & 2022, Euro 2020 & 2024): distance, goal-mouth angle, header, direct free kick. Penalties = 0.76. A central 11 m shot is 0.16 with the foot, 0.06 headed. xA. A shot links to the same team's completed pass or cross from the previous 20 s whose receiver is the shooter. If shooter or receiver numbers are missing, it suggests the last completed pass and you can untick it. That pass gets the shot's xG as xA and counts as a key pass. xT (expected threat). A 16 × 12 grid fitted on the same 230 StatsBomb matches (412,000 open-play passes and carries; set pieces excluded). Each cell holds the chance that having the ball there leads to a goal. A completed pass or cross earns xT(end) − xT(start); lost passes earn 0. Progressive pass. A completed pass that brings the ball at least 30 m closer to goal inside your own half, 15 m when it crosses halfway, or 10 m inside the opponent's half. Pass into the box. A completed pass or cross that ends in the penalty area and started outside it. PPDA (approximation). Opponent passes in their own 60% of the pitch ÷ your tackles, interceptions and fouls in that same zone. Lower = harder pressing. Built only from the events you tag (no pressure events), so compare it between your own matches, not with professional data providers. Field tilt. Your share of both teams' passes made in the final third. It shows who is pinning whom back. Possession (pass share). Your share of all passes and crosses. A standard stand-in when you don't have ball-in-play time.
